# Intelligent Biomechanics Lab

This repository contains the official website of the **Intelligent Biomechanics Lab** at **Kennesaw State University**, led by **Dr. Lei Shi**.

We combine experimental biomechanics, computational modeling, medical imaging, and machine learning to study soft tissue and organ mechanics. Our research includes patient-specific digital twins for gastrointestinal and cardiovascular applications.

**[Visit the lab website](https://www.intelligentbiomechanicslab.net)**

## Research Areas

- Machine learning for biomechanical modeling and simulation
- Multiscale and multiphysics simulation
- Constitutive modeling of soft tissues
- Experimental biomechanics and mechanical testing

## About the Website

The website introduces our team, research projects, publications, lab news, and tutorials. It also provides information for prospective students and research collaborators.
